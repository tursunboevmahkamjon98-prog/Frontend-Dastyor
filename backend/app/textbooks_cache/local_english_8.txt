UNIT 1 
LESSON 1 
Фан: Забони англисӣ 
Синф: 8 
Мавзӯъ: Starting on a Trip 
Вақт: 45 дақиқа 
Competence: 
Pupils should read the given text accurately, understand the meaning of the text and retell it. 
Салоҳият: 
Хонандагон бояд матни додашударо бехато хонанд, маънои онро фаҳмида, нақл карда тавонанд. 
Мақсади дарс: 
– ташаккул додани малакаи хондан ва фаҳмидани матн 
– инкишофи нутқи шифоҳӣ тавассути нақл (retelling) 
– ғанӣ гардондани захираи луғавии хонандагон 
Малакаҳо (Skills): 
Reading, Speaking, Writing, Vocabulary 
Таҷҳизот: 
Китоби дарсӣ, тахта, маркер/бор, дафтар, кортҳои луғат 
I. Organization Moment ( 3 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Good morning, dear pupils! 
How are you today? 
Who is on duty today? 
Who is absent? 
Let’s start our lesson. 
Students: 
Good morning, teacher! 
We are fine, thank you. 
II. Warm-up (5 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Let’s talk about travelling. 
Do you like travelling? 
Have you ever travelled by air? 
Students: 
Answer orally. 
III. Pre-reading (Vocabulary) ( 7 minutes) 
Teacher: Read and repeat. Translate the words. 
New Vocabulary: 
ticket — чипта 
booking-office — хазина, касса 
leave — тарк кардан 
clerk — мансабдор 
liner — ҳавопаймои калон 
sharp — аниқ, тез 
packing — борпечонӣ 
airport — фурудгоҳ 
Task: Make 2 sentences with the words “ticket” and “airport”. 
IV . While-reading (Reading the text) (10 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Now read the text attentively. One or two pupils read the text aloud.

STARTING ON A TRIP 
I am leaving our town for Khujand. That is already settled. I am going there by air and the ticket is in my 
pocket. This morning I’ve been to the booking-office. I didn’t know when I would leave. In the ticket 
office I asked the clerk for a seat on a TU-154 airplane. I also found out when the plane would leave. It 
leaves at 12 sharp. So I have an hour and a half for packing. But I’ll do it very quickly. As a matter of 
fact, I have only one suitcase to pack. I wanted to go to Khujand last year, but I couldn’t. Oh, it’s already 
half past ten! The airport is quite a long way from home. 
Луғат аз матн (English – Tajik): 
leave — тарк кардан 
ticket — чипта 
booking-office — касса 
clerk — мансабдор 
airplane — ҳавопаймо 
suitcase — ҷомадон 
airport — фурудгоҳ 
V . Reading Comprehension (6 minutes) 
Teacher: Answer the questions. 
1. Where is the boy going? 
2. How is he travelling? 
3. When does the plane leave? 
4. Why must he pack quickly? 
Students: 
Answer in short sentences. 
VI. Speaking (Pair work) ( 5 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Work in pairs and retell the text. 
VII. Writing (5 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Write 5–6 sentences about your trip or travelling. 
VIII. Grammar (Interrogative form) (5 min utes) 
Teacher: Put into interrogative form. 
1. He is leaving for Khujand. 
2. The plane leaves at 12 sharp. 
3. He has a ticket. 
4. The airport is far from home. 
IX. Additional T ext (September) (3 minutes) 
SEPTEMBER 
September comes from the Latin word “septem” which means seven. In today’s calendar, September is 
the ninth month. But in the old Roman calendar it was the seventh month. The Anglo-Saxons called it 
“Barley Month”. 
Луғат: 
September — сентябр 
calendar — тақвим 
month — моҳ 
Latin — лотинӣ 
X. Conclusion and Homewo rk (6 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Today we read the text, learned new words and retold the story. Good job! 
HOMEWORK: 
1. Retell the text “Starting on a Trip” (6–8 sentences). 
2. Learn the new vocabulary.

New Vocabulary: 
to suggest — пешниҳод кардан

to proceed — давом додан

Task: Make 2 sentences with the words “job” and “salary”. 
IV . While-reading (Reading the text) ( 10 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Read the text attentively. One or two pupils read aloud. 
PROFESSIONS, JOBS 
When a person finishes school or university, he starts his career. It’s not easy to choose the right 
profession. It might also be hard to find a good job with a high salary. 
I always believed that you should choose the profession which is interesting to you. It is impossible to 
achieve success in something that you don’t like. If you have to do something that is boring or makes you 
unhappy day after day, your life becomes miserable. 
I think that one of the reasons why a lot of people are poor and depressed is because they work only for 
money. Even if it is good money, but the job is very hard, eventually you would start to hate your job. 
It’s not important what you do, but it’s a lot more important if you get joy and energy from what you do. 
Луғат аз мавзӯъ (English – Tajik): 
profession — касб 
career — фаъолияти корӣ 
salary — маош 
job — кор 
success — муваффақият 
boring — дилгиркунанда 
happy — хушбахт

to ski — a skier

teachers — at school 
doctors, nurses — in the hospital 
postmen — at the post office 
miners — at the mine 
farmers — in the fields 
clerks — at the office

IX. Grammar and Writing ( 6 minutes) 
Teacher: Complete the sentences. 
I do not want to be a clerk, but I want to be a farmer. 
I don’t want to be a teacher of French, but … 
My brother didn’t want to be a mechanic, but … 
X. Dialogue (Pair work) ( 5 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Read and act the dialogue. Then make your own one. 
A: What’s your profession? 
B: I am a driver. 
A: What else can you do? 
B: I can work as a turner too. 
XI. Additional T ext (Logic Game) (5 minutes)

Read and memorise the text with numbers. 
(Instructions with double, add, multiply, subtract.)

3. Learn the new vocabulary.

– инкишофи нутқи шифоҳӣ тавассути саволу ҷавоб ва нақлкунӣ 
– васеъ намудани захираи луғавӣ аз рӯйи мавзӯъ 
– тарбия намудани ҳисси меҳнатдӯстӣ, инсондӯстӣ ва ҳамдардӣ 
Малакаҳо (Skills): 
Reading, Speaking, Writing, Vocabulary, Grammar 
Таҷҳизот: 
Китоби дарсӣ, тахта, маркер/бор, дафтар, кортҳои луғат, расм/сурат (шаҳри Verona ё ду писар)

Good morning, teacher!

Task: Make 2 sentences with the words “strawberries” and “fountain”. 
IV. While-reading (Reading the text) (12 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Read the text attentively. One or two pupils read aloud. 
TWO GENTLEMEN OF VERONA 
It was summer. A friend and I were driving from Switzerland to the lovely old Italian city of Verona. Just 
outside the city, two small boys stopped us. They were selling strawberries that looked delicious against 
the green leaves in the boy’s baskets. “Don’t buy them,” our driver said. “You will find better 
strawberries. Besides ...” His face showed that he did not like the two boys. It was true that their clothes 
were dirty and poor-looking, but as we looked at those two thin children with their serious dark eyes, we 
wanted to know more about them. My friend spoke to the boys and found out that they were brothers. 
Nicola was thirteen. Jacopo, the smaller one, was almost twelve. We bought their biggest basket of 
strawberries and then continued towards Verona. The next morning, coming out of the hotel, we saw the 
two boys again. They were beside the fountain in the public square, shining the shoes of men who passed 
by. They were very busy, but they said “hello” to us in a friendly way. “I thought you sold strawberries,” 
I said. “We do many things, sir,” Nicola answered seriously. He looked at us hopefully. “We sometimes 
guide people through the city, showing them places of interest.” “All right,” I smiled. “You can guide us. 
Let’s go.” As they guided us through the city, I continued to study these boys. They were children, yes, 
and they acted like children in many ways. But their faces were serious, and they both had an appearance 
of purpose that one does not often find in small boys. (Adapted from Reader’s Digest Readings.) 
Луғат аз мавзӯъ (English – Tajik):

basket — сабад 
dirty — ифлос 
brothers — бародарон 
to shine shoes — пойафзол тоза кардан 
public square — майдони умумӣ 
to guide — роҳнамоӣ кардан 
places of interest — ҷойҳои ҷолиб 
V. Reading Comprehension (6 minutes)

Example prompts:

VIII. Homework (3 minutes)

Teacher:

Мавзӯъ: My Friend’s Story (My Flat) 
Вақт: 45 дақиқа 
Competence: 
Pupils should read the given text accurately, understand the meaning of the text and retell it. 
Салоҳият: 
Хонандагон бояд матни додашударо бехато хонанд, маънои онро фаҳмида, нақл карда тавонанд. 
Мақсади дарс: 
– ташаккул додани малакаи хондан ва фаҳмидани матн дар бораи хона/квартира 
– инкишофи нутқи шифоҳӣ дар тасвир кардани ҳуҷраҳо ва ашёҳо 
– васеъ намудани луғат дар мавзӯи “flat / rooms / furniture” 
– мустаҳкам намудани грамматика (Present Continuous, Past Indefinite, Wh-questions) 
Малакаҳо (Skills): 
Reading, Speaking, Writing, Vocabulary, Grammar 
Таҷҳизот: 
Китоби дарсӣ, тахта, маркер/бор, дафтар, кортҳои луғат, расмҳои хона/ҳуҷра 
I. Organization Moment (3 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Good morning, dear pupils! 
How are you today? 
Who is on duty today? 
Who is absent? 
Students: 
Good morning, teacher! 
We are fine, thank you. 
II. Warm-up (5 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Let’s talk about your home. 
Do you live in a flat or a house? 
How many rooms are there in your home? 
Students: 
Answer orally. 
III. Pre-reading (Vocabulary) (8 minutes) 
Teacher: Read and repeat. Translate the words. 
New Vocabulary: 
to have a house-warming — чашни хонадароӣ гузарондан 
modern conveniences — шароити замонавӣ 
air conditioning — ҳавои мувофиқ (кондиционер) 
to say nothing — чизе нагуфтан 
vacuum cleaner — мошинаи гарду чанг ҷамъкунанда 
running water — оби равон 
central heating — гармкунии марказӣ 
lift — лифт 
block of flats — бинои бисёрошёна 
Task: Make 2 sentences with “flat” and “lift”.

IV. While-reading (Reading the text) (10 minutes) 
Teacher: 
Read the text attentively. One or two pupils read aloud. 
MY FRIEND’S STORY 
A few weeks ago our family moved to a new flat and gave a house-warming party. Our new flat is fine 
and we all like it very much. The flat consists of three rooms, a kitchen, a bathroom and a toilet (a 
lavatory). All the modern conveniences are in our flat: running water, gas, rubbish chute, air conditioning, 
to say nothing of electricity and central heating. My granny always gives me some work to do in the 
house. It may be dusting, shaking and beating the mats and carpets, or cleaning them with the vacuum 
cleaner, polishing the floor, going to the shop and so on. We live on the fifth floor. There is a shop on the 
ground floor. There is a lift in our block of flats. We live in Rudaki street in a new block of flats. 
Луғат аз мавзӯъ (English – Tajik): 
flat — квартира/хона 
room — ҳуҷра 
kitchen — ошхона 
bathroom — ҳаммом 
toilet — ҳоҷатхона 
conveniences — шароитҳо 
work — кор 
to dust — чанголудан 
carpet — қолин 
floor — фарш 
shop — мағоза 
V. Grammar Practice (7 minutes) 
Teacher: Read and translate these verbs. Make up sentences in Present Continuous and Past Indefinite. 
to break — broke — broken — breaking 
to bring — brought — brought — bringing 
to build — built — built — building 
Task: Make 2 sentences: one in Present Continuous, one in Past Indefinite.

Teacher: Ask as many Wh-questions as you can to get information about these statements. 
She brought. 
He is building. 
Students: 
Make Wh-questions orally. 
VII. Dialogue (Pair work) (5 minutes) 
Teacher: Read and act this dialogue. Then make up your own dialogue. 
A: Is Mary busy at the moment? 
B: Yes, she is writing letters. 
A: And what about Henry? 
B: He is watching television. 
VIII. Writing (5 minutes) 
Teacher: Write a short composition (6–8 sentences) on “My flat (house)”. 
IX. Homework (5 minutes) 
Teacher: 
1. Write a composition on “My flat (house, veranda, kitchen, toilet, bedroom, hall)”. 
2. Learn the new vocabulary. 
3. Be ready to retell “My Friend’s Story”. 
Conclusion (5 minutes) 
Teacher:

Today we read about a flat, learned new words, practiced grammar and spoke about our homes. Well 
done!

AFTER THE THUNDER 
The thunder crashes and the first drops of rain fall on the dusty grass. A clap of thunder, another clap, and 
the rain falls heavily. The rain cools the air and lays off the dust. The grass and the foliage on the trees are 
green again, the plants are refreshed, everything looks fresh and bright. The river is glittering in the sun, 
the sea is calm. What glorious weather! And if we think that this fine weather will last we say, “We are in 
for a spell of fine weather”, or, “We are in for a fine spell”. 
Луғат аз мавзӯъ (English – Tajik): 
rain — борон
